Background & Story

About Me

Engineer, gamer, musician, tech enthusiast.

I'm a Civil Design Engineer based in Melbourne with a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) from Macquarie University and GradIEAust status with Engineers Australia. Born in Bangladesh, raised in Sydney, now living in Melbourne — engineering has always been my way of connecting technical skill with real-world impact.

🏗️
The Engineer

Civil Design Engineer with hands-on experience in structural assessments, telecommunications infrastructure upgrades, and low-carbon construction research. Proficient in AutoCAD, Civil 3D, Revit, SPACE GASS, RS2, and Slide2. GradIEAust member of Engineers Australia.

💻
The Tech Enthusiast

Alongside engineering, I've built a parallel track in insurance technology at Suncorp Group — working across Salesforce, Tableau, ServiceNow, and Citrix to drive process improvements and data-led decisions. I'm mentored by the Head of Digital Customer, and actively developing toward Business Analysis and Product Management roles at the intersection of engineering and digital.
