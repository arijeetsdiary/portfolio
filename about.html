About Me

Engineer, gamer, musician, collector.

I'm a civil engineer based in Australia with a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) from Macquarie University. Born in Bangladesh, raised in Sydney, now living in Melbourne — engineering has always been my way of connecting technical skill with real-world impact.
